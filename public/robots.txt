# AMRAP With Friends — https://amrapwithfriends.com

# Everything public is open to everyone. Private and ephemeral surfaces are kept
# out of the index with X-Robots-Tag headers (see middleware.ts), not with
# Disallow rules, so crawlers can still follow a shared rally link and render a
# social card for it.
#
# The named groups below are redundant with the wildcard today. They exist so the
# policy for each AI agent is explicit and reviewable in one place, and so a
# future rule cannot silently change what an AI crawler is allowed to fetch.
